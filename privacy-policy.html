Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Pizza di Casa, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (the "Site").

1. Data Controller

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK data protection laws, the Data Controller is:
Ajmain
Operating in Oxford/London, UK
Email: tuaemail@esempio.com

2. How We Collect and Use Your Data

Our website is primarily an informational portfolio. We do not use internal contact forms, user accounts, or databases to collect personal information automatically.

  • Voluntary Contact: If you choose to contact us for catering quotes or inquiries by clicking our email links or the WhatsApp button, you will be redirected to your own email client or the WhatsApp application. The personal data you share (such as your name, phone number, and email address) will only be used to respond to your specific inquiry and fulfill our services.
  • No Internal Tracking: We do not track your browsing behavior internally or sell your data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services and Embeds

To showcase our story and events, we embed content from social media platforms. Loading these specific pages may result in third parties collecting your data.

  • Instagram: Our "Our Story" page features embedded content from Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited). When you view this content, Instagram may collect your IP address, browser type, and interaction data, and may set cookies on your device in accordance with their own policies. For more information, please review the Instagram Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Security

Any personal data sent to us directly via email or WhatsApp is kept strictly confidential and retained only for as long as necessary to provide our catering services, fulfill legal obligations, or resolve disputes. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to secure your communications with us.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request the correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request the deletion of your personal data (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Object to the processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address provided above.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
