Menu & Services

Our Menu

  • Margherita – San Marzano tomato sauce, fior di latte mozzarella, fresh basil, and extra virgin olive oil.
  • Pizza al Portafoglio (Wallet Pizza) – The original folded pizza from Italy. Simple, authentic, and made to go.
  • Nutella Pizza – For the sweet tooth! A warm pizza base smothered in rich Nutella.
  • Vegetarian & Vegan Options – Fresh seasonal vegetables and dairy-free alternatives available.
  • Drinks – A selection of soft drinks and water.

...and more! Ask us about special toppings or off-menu requests based on seasonal ingredients.

Where to find us

We regularly pitch our stall at the Gloucester Green Market in Oxford and the Oxford Outdoor Market. Check our Instagram @pizza.di.casa.1 for our weekly schedule and exclusive discounts!
