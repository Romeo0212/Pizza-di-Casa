About Pizza di Casa

"My passion for pizza started after I moved to the UK and realised how much I missed the taste of a good, authentic Italian pizza. I started from the basics, spending countless hours learning about dough, fermentation, and hydration to get the perfect bake."

What started as a personal passion became something much bigger: Pizza di Casa, an Italian street-food stall bringing fresh, homemade-style pizza to markets, festivals, and private events across England.
